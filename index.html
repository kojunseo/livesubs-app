Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests? Please open an issue at github.com/kojunseo/livesubs-app/issues. We usually reply within a few days.

How to use

  1. Choose the language spoken in the video and the language you want captions in.
  2. Download the on-device models once (speech recognition and translation).
  3. Tap Start Live Captions and start the screen broadcast for LiveSubs.
  4. Open any video app. Captions appear in a floating Picture in Picture window.

FAQ

Is my audio sent anywhere?

No. Transcription and translation run entirely on your device. LiveSubs has no servers and collects no data.

Why don't captions appear for some apps?

Some apps protect their audio (for example DRM-protected streaming), so iOS does not share it with screen broadcasts.

Do I need a subscription?

No. LiveSubs is a one-time purchase with no subscription, ads or account.

사용 방법: 영상 언어와 자막 언어 선택 → 기기 내 모델 다운로드(최초 1회) → ‘실시간 자막 시작’을 누르고 LiveSubs 화면 방송 시작 → 영상 앱으로 이동하면 PiP 창에 자막이 떠요.

받아쓰기와 번역은 모두 기기 안에서 이뤄지며, 서버로 어떤 데이터도 전송되지 않아요. 일부 앱(DRM 보호 콘텐츠 등)은 오디오를 보호하기 때문에 자막이 나오지 않을 수 있어요.
